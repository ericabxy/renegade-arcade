As part of my arcade project, I intend to play and document every open-source game I find interesting. Well-known classic video games are the subject of many fansites and communities, and I think the open-source classics deserve their time in the limelight as well.

[Roguebox Adventures](https://rogueboxadventures.tuxfamily.org/) (RBA) is a sandbox role-playing adventure game developed by [The Mighty Glider](https://themightyglider.itch.io/) in the mid-to-late 2010s. Its latest releases---3.0.0 and 3.0.1---appear to have been published in 2020. RBA is written in Python 3 using the [Pygame](https://www.pygame.org/wiki/about) library. There appears to have been some effort to port it to the GCW, but it is mainly a personal computer game due to its dependencies. However, it appears to run smoothly on my Pinebook Pro making it a promising portable game for low-end laptops.

Many of the download links for RBA are currently non-functional (as of 2025) due to technical issues with <tuxfamily.org>, but the developers have an undocumented repository at Gitlab. As such, the official links to download standalone releases for [RBA at Itchio](https://themightyglider.itch.io/roguebox-adventures) are broken and players should download the game in source form from the [RBA Gitlab](https://gitlab.com/The_Mighty_Glider/rba).
