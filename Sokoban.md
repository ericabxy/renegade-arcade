Sokoban
-------

Sokoban is a transport puzzle video game developed by Thinking Rabbit in 1982. It is the subject of many game programming tutorials thanks to its simple mechanics leading to complex gameplay.

- [XSokoban](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/andru/xsokoban.html)
- [Sokoban tutorial by Berbasoft](https://berbasoft.com/simplegametutorials/love/sokoban/)
