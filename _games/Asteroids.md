Asteroids
---------

The Atari arcade classic _Asteroids_ is subject the subject of many beginner-level tutorials thanks to its familiar game mechanics and challenges. Die-on-contact is one of the simplest collision detection and resolution systems, and asteroids that break up into smaller pieces when they are destroyed are a fun programming challenge and player challenge at the same time.
- [Vectoroids](http://www.newbreedsoftware.com/vectoroids/)
- [Asteroids tutorial by Berbasoft](https://berbasoft.com/simplegametutorials/love/asteroids/)
